# Gemini Video concept prompt — Nebulus × Blue Lotus

Use this prompt to generate a visual-development clip. The result is a motion
reference for a browser webcam VFX demo, not a final asset to ship directly.

## Main prompt

Create a 16:9, 12-second cinematic concept video for an interactive live webcam
magic performance in a modern classroom. One student stands naturally in front
of a static camera. Keep the student's face, body, clothes, hands, room, camera
angle, and framing consistent for the entire shot. The camera never cuts.

The effect has one continuous cause-and-effect sequence:

### Phase 1 — touch and draw, 0.0–3.2 seconds

A small, subtle activation zone appears near the lower-left third of the
screen. It looks like a faint pool of floating dust, not a UI button and not a
magic circle. The student places only the tip of one index finger inside that
zone and begins drawing a long, smooth curved line through the air.

The glowing line must grow exactly behind the moving fingertip. It must never
lead the finger. The line is narrow and soft, with an ivory-white core and a
very restrained pale cyan edge. Along the line, emit:

- fine magic dust with varied particle sizes;
- soft translucent smoke wisps with low opacity;
- a few slow glitter points;
- gentle turbulence and curl;
- gradual fading at the oldest end of the path.

The trail must feel like smoke-written calligraphy in three-dimensional air,
not a neon tube, laser, vector stroke, rope, lightning bolt, or solid ribbon.

### Phase 2 — threshold and rising fog, 3.2–6.0 seconds

When the accumulated drawn path becomes long enough, the trail gives one soft
pulse. This is the visible threshold confirmation. Do not explode.

Fog begins rising from several points along the completed path, especially from
its oldest section. The fog should grow upward and outward in multiple
overlapping volumes, then merge into a wide atmospheric layer across the full
camera frame. Preserve transparency: the student and classroom must remain
visible through the fog. Avoid black plates, rectangular video boundaries,
opaque grey blobs, cotton-ball clouds, or a dark overlay that crushes the
camera image.

The fog is neutral pearl-grey. Optional pink or blue identity is visible only
as sparse, tiny glints. Never tint the full fog cloud bright pink or blue.

### Phase 3 — summon the lotus, 6.0–8.8 seconds

The student opens both palms and holds them facing one another. A glassy blue
lotus gradually forms exactly between the two palms. The flower has curved,
soft petals, fine natural veins, a cyan-to-deep-blue material gradient, subtle
subsurface scattering, and restrained internal glow. It is not plastic,
metallic, toy-like, or a flat sprite.

The lotus opens with organic petal deformation. Petal tips curl outward.
The flower gently breathes and rotates in real three-dimensional perspective.
Fog parts slightly around the lotus because of its energy.

### Phase 4 — lotus release and petal rain, 8.8–12.0 seconds

The lotus compresses inward for a brief anticipation beat, flashes from its
core, then releases a layered pressure burst. Do not use a generic fire
explosion. The flower separates into many individual blue petals.

Petals first move outward from the burst, then lose speed, catch air, and fall
slowly across the entire screen. Use varied depth, scale, focus, spin, drift,
and falling speed. Some petals cross in front of the student, some fall behind,
and some pass close to the camera with shallow depth of field. By the final
frame, blue petals are still falling lazily throughout the full composition
while the pearl-grey fog continues drifting.

## Visual direction

- Premium feature-film fantasy compositing grounded in real camera footage.
- Physically plausible light wrap on hands, face, clothes, and nearby fog.
- Many low-opacity layers instead of a few large sprites.
- Clear anticipation, activation, sustain, release, and aftermath phases.
- Natural inertia and drag for every particle and petal.
- Effects must respond to the fingertip and palms with no visible tracking lag.
- Keep the classroom readable; do not replace the background.

## Strict exclusions

No swords. No lightning. No fireball. No comet. No energy orb. No weather
buttons. No runic portal. No circular HUD. No giant magic circle. No anime
speed lines. No opaque black smoke. No rectangular overlay boundary. No
cartoon clouds. No childish sparkles. No random effects that were not caused by
the student's finger path or two-palm lotus gesture.

## Optional generation settings

- Duration: 12 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Camera: locked-off medium-wide webcam framing
- Motion strength: medium
- Stylization: low
- Realism: high
- Prompt adherence: high

## What to inspect after Gemini generates the clip

1. Does the trail begin at the activation zone and remain attached to the
   fingertip?
2. Is the path-length threshold visually understandable without text?
3. Does fog rise from the path instead of appearing from nowhere?
4. Can the classroom remain visible through the fog?
5. Does the lotus sit exactly between both palms?
6. Do petals burst outward first, then fall with drag across the entire frame?
7. Are any forbidden legacy effects visible?
